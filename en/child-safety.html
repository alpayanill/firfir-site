Child Safety Standards

FirFir has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM). These standards apply to every part of the app: profiles, photos, messages, matches, topic channels, events and the BFF AI companion.

1. Adults only (18+)

FirFir is only for people aged 18 and over. Date of birth is required at sign-up and people under 18 cannot create an account. Accounts we believe belong to someone under 18 are closed.

2. Prohibited content and behaviour

Any content or behaviour that sexualises, grooms, exploits or endangers children is strictly prohibited. This includes child sexual abuse material, sexual messages to or about children, attempts to contact children, and sharing or requesting such material. Violations result in immediate and permanent account removal.

3. In-app reporting

Every profile, chat, channel message, event and BFF conversation has a "Report" option, and users can block anyone. Child safety reports are prioritised and reviewed by our team within 24 hours.

4. Moderation and enforcement

Our team reviews reports, removes violating content, suspends or permanently bans accounts, and preserves relevant information as required by law. Abusive language in messages is masked by an automated filter.

5. Reporting to authorities

When we become aware of child sexual abuse material or a child at risk, we report it to the competent authorities: in Turkey to the Information and Communication Technologies Authority hotline (ihbarweb.org.tr) and law enforcement, and internationally to NCMEC. We cooperate with investigations.

6. Child safety point of contact

For any child safety concern or report:
