Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains what data we collect while you use FirFir, how we protect it, and your rights.

1. Data Controller

Personal data collected within the FirFir application is processed by Alpay Anıl Yenihayat (“FirFir”) as the data controller, in accordance with the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK). You can contact the data controller at info@firfirapp.com.

The app is intended for users aged 18 and over; we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

2. Data We Collect

While you use the app, we collect the following data:

  • Account information: your email address and password (passwords are stored as irreversible hashes). If you sign in with Google, Apple or Microsoft, the email address and account ID that provider shares with us.
  • Profile information: name, date of birth, height, weight, gender, the gender you are interested in, job, education, about text and interests.
  • Photos: your profile and gallery photos; the photo you choose for your BFF.
  • Profile verification: if you choose to verify your profile, a selfie taken with a requested pose and the record of your consent. A one-way fingerprint (hash) of your photos and selfie is kept to detect copies; it cannot be turned back into an image.
  • Invitations: the invite code you share or use, who invited you and which invited friends verified their profiles.
  • Usage purpose and preferences: why you use the app (friendship, travel, sports, etc.), your match filters and your account settings.
  • Location data: your current location while the app is open, if you have granted location permission.
  • Messages and posts: your direct messages, what you write in event and category chats, and the events you create.
  • AI companion (BFF) data: your conversations with your BFFs and the notes derived from them. These notes may include health-related information such as medication, sleep and mood (see Section 5).
  • Purchase information: your premium plan, store transaction ID, purchase and expiry dates. Payment and card details are handled by the App Store / Google Play and never reach us.
  • Report and block records: reports you make and reports made about you, and the users you block.
  • Device and technical information: notification token, platform and app language; IP address and request time in server logs kept for security.
  • Usage statistics: how much time you spend in each part of the app, how often you open it, the app version and platform, and your steps around Premium offers and the Premium screen (an offer being shown, the screen being opened, purchase or restore attempts and their outcome, and where you came from). These statistics are linked to your account and grouped by the gender in your profile; no message content or location is collected for this.
  • Crash reports: when the app crashes or an unexpected error occurs, the error log, device model, operating system and app version. These reports are not linked to your identity.

3. Why We Process Your Data

We process your data only for the following purposes:

  • Creating your account, verifying your email address and managing your account,
  • Verifying, at your request, that your profile photos belong to you (manual review of a posed selfie) and showing the blue check; running the invite program and granting its free premium rewards,
  • Suggesting matches that fit your usage purpose and preferences,
  • Showing nearby users and events,
  • Running messaging, event and chat room features,
  • Providing the BFF AI companion (generating replies, sending reminders and follow-up messages),
  • Verifying and managing your premium subscription,
  • Sending match, message and reminder notifications,
  • Improving the app: measuring how its parts are used and how Premium offers perform, and finding and fixing errors,
  • Reviewing reports, blocking objectionable content, keeping the app secure and preventing abuse.

Your data is never sold to third parties. Non-Premium accounts see ads inside the app; the data processed for ads is described in section 7.

4. Location Data

If you have granted location permission, your location is sent to our servers at regular intervals while the app is open. It is used to calculate distances in Discover and to show nearby events. Your location is not collected while the app is closed or in the background.

You appear on the map only when you turn on map (market) mode; other users then see your approximate distance and map position. No location history is kept; only your last known location is stored.

To stop sending your location, revoke the app's location permission in your device settings.

5. AI Companion (BFF)

Your BFF is not a real person but an AI-powered chat companion; it does not give medical, legal or financial advice. We ask for your explicit consent before you use BFF for the first time. If you do not consent, you cannot use BFF; the rest of the app is not affected.

  • Replies are generated by AI models, which may run on our own servers or at contracted AI service providers; your messages are processed for this purpose.
  • So that your BFF can remember you, notes are derived from your conversations (e.g. medication times, sleep patterns, mood, goals, important events). Such health-related information may qualify as special category personal data under KVKK; it is processed only with your explicit consent and stored encrypted.
  • When you ask about something current, such as a match result, the weather or exchange rates, a short search query derived from your message is sent to a search service provider. Your full conversation, your name and your account details are not sent.
  • In voice chat, your speech is converted to text by your device's speech recognition service (Apple on iOS, Google on Android); your voice recording is never sent to or stored on our servers.
  • For safety, report handling and service quality, BFF conversations may be reviewed by our authorized team, who may step into a conversation when necessary.

You can view notes in the Notes section of the BFF screen and delete them one by one. To have all of your BFF data deleted, write to us at the contact address below or delete your account.

6. Data Security

Your personal data is stored encrypted at the field (column) level in our databases using AES-256-GCM. Even in the event of unauthorized database access, your personal data remains unreadable.

All data transmission happens over encrypted connections (TLS/HTTPS). Your passwords are never stored in plain text.

7. Data Sharing and International Transfers

Your personal data is never shared with third parties for marketing and is never sold. To provide the service, data is shared only with the following service providers, and only as far as necessary:

  • Hosting: all of your data is stored and processed on our servers in Hetzner Online GmbH's data center in Finland (European Union).
  • Google Firebase (Google LLC): your device notification token for push notifications; your email address and account ID to authenticate Google, Apple or Microsoft sign-in.
  • Firebase Crashlytics (Google LLC): crash reports (error log, device model, operating system and app version); not linked to your identity.
  • Sign in with Apple, Google and Microsoft: when you use these methods, your identity is verified by the respective provider.
  • AI and search service providers: your messages, to generate BFF replies, and search queries derived from current-information questions in BFF (Section 5). We use contracted providers that process this data on our behalf and only for this purpose; our current provider is Google LLC. If the provider changes, we will update this section.
  • App Store (Apple) and Google Play (Google): purchases are processed by the store; transaction information is shared with the store to verify your subscription.
  • Email delivery service: your email address, to deliver your verification code.
  • OpenStreetMap: map images are downloaded by your device directly from OpenStreetMap Foundation servers; your IP address and the map area you view are sent to those servers in the process.
  • Google AdMob (Google LLC): for ads shown to non-Premium accounts, your device's advertising identifier, IP address, device and app information and approximate location are sent to Google. Ads are shown without personalisation; you can reset your advertising identifier in your device settings or remove ads entirely by going Premium. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland your consent is requested through Google's consent form before any ads are shown (a legally required notice is also shown in applicable US states); you can change your choice at any time under Profile → Account Settings → Ad privacy options.
  • If a legal obligation arises, data may be shared with competent public authorities, limited strictly to what is requested.

Because these providers' servers are located outside Türkiye (in the European Union, the United Kingdom and the USA), your data is transferred abroad. Such transfers are carried out in accordance with Article 9 of KVKK.

8. Retention and Deletion

Your data is kept for as long as your account remains active. You can permanently delete your account under Profile → Account Settings → Delete Account. Your profile information, photos, location, matches, messages, events, BFF conversations and notes, and subscription records are then deleted from all of our systems; if you signed in with Apple, your Sign in with Apple authorization is also revoked.

Server security logs (IP address, request time) are deleted after a limited period. Usage statistics are deleted after 100 days; crash reports are kept in Firebase Crashlytics for 90 days. Store purchase records are kept by Apple and Google under their own policies.

Your verification selfie is used only for the review: it is deleted once the review is finished, and a selfie that is never submitted is deleted within 24 hours. Only its one-way hash is kept for copy detection, and it is deleted together with your account.

9. Your Rights Under KVKK

Under Article 11 of KVKK, you have the right to:

  • Learn whether your personal data is being processed,
  • Request information about such processing,
  • Learn the purpose of processing and whether data is used accordingly,
  • Request correction of incomplete or inaccurate data,
  • Request deletion or destruction of your data,
  • Object to a result against you arising from the analysis of your processed data exclusively through automated systems,
  • Claim compensation for damages caused by unlawful processing.

To exercise these rights, contact us at the address below.

10. Users in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland (GDPR)

If you are in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the UK GDPR and the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection also apply to your personal data, and this section applies to you in addition to the rest of this policy.

We rely on the following legal bases:

  • Contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR): creating and managing your account, matching and Discover, the map, messaging, events and chat rooms, and providing and verifying your subscriptions.
  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR): the gender you are interested in, which may reveal your sexual orientation (asked at registration); the BFF AI companion and the health-related notes derived from your conversations; profile verification with a selfie; push notifications; and ads, through Google's consent form.
  • Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR): keeping the app and accounts secure, preventing fraud and abuse, moderating content and handling reports, diagnosing crashes and errors, and measuring how the app is used so that we can improve it.
  • Legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR): keeping purchase records and responding to lawful requests from authorities.

Information that may reveal your sexual orientation and health information you share with BFF are special categories of personal data. We process them only with your explicit consent (Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR), which we ask for separately at registration and before you first use BFF.

You have the right to:

  • access your personal data and receive a copy of it,
  • have inaccurate data rectified,
  • have your data erased,
  • restrict processing,
  • receive the data you provided to us in a structured, machine-readable format and have it transmitted to another controller (portability),
  • object to processing, including processing based on our legitimate interests,
  • withdraw your consent at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out before the withdrawal,
  • not be subject to decisions based solely on automated processing that produce legal or similarly significant effects for you. We do not make such decisions: suspensions and bans are always decided by a person.

To withdraw a consent, turn off push notifications in your device settings, change your ad choice under Profile → Account Settings → Ad privacy options or, for the other consents, write to us or delete your account.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the data protection supervisory authority of the country where you live or work, or where an alleged infringement took place.

International transfers: your data is stored and processed in the European Union (Finland). Where data is transferred to Google LLC in the USA (push notifications, sign-in, crash reports, ads and BFF's current-information search), the transfer relies on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (including its UK and Swiss extensions) and/or the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

To exercise your rights, write to info@firfirapp.com, preferably from the email address linked to your account. We reply within one month; if a request is complex, this period may be extended by up to two further months, and we will let you know. You can also edit your profile, delete your BFF notes and delete your account directly in the app.

11. US State Privacy Rights

If you live in a US state with a consumer privacy law, such as California (CCPA, as amended by the CPRA), Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, Utah, Texas or Oregon, this section applies to you in addition to the rest of this policy.

In the last 12 months we have collected the following categories of personal information:

  • Identifiers: name, email address, account ID, IP address, notification token and advertising identifier.
  • Personal records: your account and profile information (such as height, weight, job and education) and the messages and content you share.
  • Characteristics: age (date of birth) and gender.
  • Commercial information: your Premium purchases and subscriptions.
  • App activity: your matches, likes, settings, usage statistics, and crash and diagnostic data.
  • Precise geolocation: your location while the app is open, if you allow it.
  • Photos: your profile and gallery photos and your verification selfie.
  • Sensitive personal information: precise geolocation; information that may reveal your sexual orientation (the gender you are interested in); health information you share with BFF; and your account login (email address and password).

We collect this information from you, from your device, from other users (for example, reports about you) and, if you use them, from Google, Apple or Microsoft sign-in. We use it for the purposes in section 3 and keep it for the periods in section 8. We disclose it for business purposes only to the service providers in section 7, which process it on our behalf, and to authorities where the law requires it.

We do not sell personal information. To show ads in non-Premium accounts, we share device identifiers, approximate location and ad interaction data with Google AdMob, which some state laws treat as "sharing" or targeted advertising. You can opt out under Profile → Account Settings → Ad privacy options or by writing to us, or remove ads entirely with Premium.

We use sensitive personal information only to provide the service you request (for example, showing distances, matching you by the gender you are interested in and personalising BFF) and to keep the app secure, never to infer characteristics about you. Where state law requires it, we ask for your consent first.

Depending on your state, you have the right to:

  • know what personal information we have collected about you and access a copy of it,
  • delete it,
  • correct inaccurate information,
  • receive it in a portable format,
  • opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information and of targeted advertising,
  • limit the use of your sensitive personal information,
  • not be discriminated against for exercising these rights.

To make a request, email info@firfirapp.com from the email address linked to your account; we verify requests through that address. You may also use an authorized agent; we will then ask for proof that the agent is authorized to act for you. We respond within 45 days. If we decline your request, we explain why, and you can appeal by replying to our decision. If your appeal is denied, you may contact your state's Attorney General.

We do not knowingly collect personal information of anyone under 18.

12. Consumer Health Data

This is our consumer health data privacy policy under the Washington My Health My Data Act and the consumer health data laws of Nevada and Connecticut. It is part of our Privacy Policy and is also published as a separate page on our website.

  • What we collect: only the health information you choose to share with BFF, your AI companion (such as your medication and medication times, sleep, mood and health goals), the notes derived from it and the parts of your BFF conversations that contain it.
  • Why: to personalise BFF's replies and to send you the reminders and follow-up messages you ask for.
  • Consent: we collect it only after you give the separate BFF consent in the app. BFF is optional; the rest of the app works without it.
  • Source: only you, through your own BFF conversations.
  • Sharing: we never sell consumer health data. We share it only with service providers that process it on our behalf: our hosting provider Hetzner Online GmbH (Finland, EU), where it is stored encrypted, and, when you ask BFF about something current, our search provider, which receives only a short search query derived from your message, without your name or account details. We do not share it with any other third party or affiliate, except where the law requires it.
  • Your rights: you can ask whether we hold consumer health data about you and access it, delete it (notes one by one in the Notes section of the BFF screen; everything by writing to us or deleting your account) and withdraw your consent at any time by writing to us. If we decline a request, you can appeal by replying to our decision.

Contact: info@firfirapp.com. We respond within 45 days.
